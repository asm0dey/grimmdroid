Grimmdroid is a native Android client for Grimmory, the self-hosted book server.

• Sign in to one or more Grimmory servers with a password or SSO
• Browse your libraries and shelves, sort and filter, and search the whole catalogue
• Download books and open them in the reader app you already use, share them, or save them to a folder
• Choose what happens after a download: nothing, open in your reader, or save to your folder
• Your library stays browsable offline, and downloaded books still open
• Light and dark themes with dynamic colour

Requires a Grimmory server with the mobile app API. Audiobooks aren't supported yet.

Grimmdroid is free software, licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later. It contains no ads, no analytics and no proprietary libraries.
